New homes

New doesn't mean perfect.

Builders work fast. An independent inspection before closing, and again before your 11-month warranty expires, catches defects while the builder still has to fix them.

Book · from $325Full price list

What we check

Pre-closing inspection

A full inspection before your final walkthrough so the punch list is complete.

11-month warranty inspection

Documents defects that showed up after move-in, before the builder's warranty ends.

Moisture & thermal

Thermal imaging is especially useful in new homes for missing insulation and hidden leaks.

We've inspected new construction townhomes and single-family homes across South Florida. The most common findings are small but add up: missing insulation, unsealed penetrations, grading and drainage issues, and incomplete finish work.

Book the 11-month inspection about a month before your warranty date so there's time to submit the list to the builder.
