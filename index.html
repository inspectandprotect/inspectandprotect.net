Mold Inspection & Testing

We find the moisture first, then test what matters.

A spore count alone can't tell you where the water is coming from. We check the home with a moisture meter and thermal camera before a single sample is taken.

Every accessible room

Walls, ceilings, floors, baseboards, under sinks, showers, kitchen and laundry.

Thermal imaging

Temperature patterns around windows, plumbing and the roofline that can point to hidden moisture.

AC system & attic

Air handler, coil, drain pan and line, duct connections and attic insulation.

Lab-analyzed samples

Up to 3 air or surface samples with an outdoor control, sent to an accredited lab.

We test. We don't sell cleanup.

We don't do mold removal, so our findings never come with a job attached.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does a home inspection cost?

Home inspections start at $325 for condos and homes under 1,200 sq ft and are priced by size. Wind mitigation and 4-point inspections are $150 each. See the full price list on our pricing page.

How long does a home inspection take?

Most inspections take 1 to 2 hours depending on the size and condition of the home. You're welcome to attend.

When will I receive my report?

Reports and photos are delivered the same day as your inspection.

What areas do you serve?

Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties. Call for availability elsewhere in Florida.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We're a Florida licensed home inspector (#HI18768), InterNACHI certified, and carry errors and omissions and general liability insurance.

Ready to book?

Call, text or book online. We confirm within 2 hours.
