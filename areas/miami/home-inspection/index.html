Home Inspection for Miami homes

From high-rise condos to older single-family homes in neighborhoods built decades ago, Miami's housing stock covers every era of Florida construction. Older homes often need a 4-point for insurance, and condo owners commonly need a wind mitigation form for their unit.

South Florida homes face a combination you don't see in most of the country: salt air, intense UV, months of humidity and a hurricane season every year. That shows up in roofs that age faster, AC systems that work harder, and water that finds its way into places you can't see from the hallway.

Every inspection includes a moisture meter and a thermal camera at no extra charge. Water damage is the most common and most expensive surprise in Florida homes, so we look for it on every job, not only when you pay for an add-on.

What we check

Roof & attic

Roof covering, flashing, visible decking, attic framing, ventilation and insulation.

Structure

Foundation, walls, ceilings, floors, windows and doors, with attention to cracks and water intrusion.

Electrical

Main panel, breakers, visible wiring, outlets, GFCI and AFCI protection.

Plumbing

Supply and drain piping, fixtures, water heater, and signs of leaks.

HVAC

Operation of the AC system, air handler, condensate line and drain pan.

Moisture & thermal

Moisture meter readings and thermal imaging on suspect areas, included on every inspection.
