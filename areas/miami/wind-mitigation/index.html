Wind Mitigation Inspection for Miami homes

From high-rise condos to older single-family homes in neighborhoods built decades ago, Miami's housing stock covers every era of Florida construction. Older homes often need a 4-point for insurance, and condo owners commonly need a wind mitigation form for their unit.

Florida law requires insurers to offer discounts for wind-resistant construction features, but they can only apply them if the features are documented on the official form. A wind mitigation inspection is how that documentation happens.

We complete the current revision of the OIR-B1-1802 form with supporting photos for every answer. Insurers reject reports on outdated form versions, so we keep up with revisions.

What we check

Building code

Year built and permit history used to determine which code the home was built to.

Roof covering

Roof type and its product approval or permit date.

Roof deck attachment

How the roof decking is fastened to the trusses or rafters.

Roof-to-wall attachment

Toe nails, clips, single or double wraps connecting the roof to the walls.

Roof geometry

Hip, gable or flat roof shape and how much of each.

Secondary water resistance

Whether a sealed underlayment would keep water out if shingles or tiles blow off.

Opening protection

Impact windows, doors, garage doors and shutters, and their ratings.

Miami wind mitigation inspection questions

How much is a wind mitigation inspection in Miami?

$150. There's no travel fee within Miami-Dade and Broward.

How long does a wind mitigation take in Miami?

Usually 30 to 45 minutes on site, with the form delivered the same day.

Will it lower my insurance?

If your home has qualifying features, your insurer applies the discounts once the form is on file. The amount depends on your home and your insurer.
