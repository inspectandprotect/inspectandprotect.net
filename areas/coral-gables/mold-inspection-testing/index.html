Mold Inspection & Testing for Coral Gables homes

Coral Gables is known for its Mediterranean Revival homes, many decades old, alongside newer construction. Older Gables homes often have barrel tile roofs, original plumbing and aging electrical, all of which matter on a 4-point.

In South Florida, the air handler often sits in a closet or attic, and the coil, drain pan and duct connections are where mold most often starts. We open up and check the system, not just the rooms.

Samples go where the findings point, not to fill a quota. An indoor air sample is always compared with an outdoor sample taken the same day, because an indoor number means little on its own.

Mold inspection and testing are performed under our Florida home inspector license. We do not perform mold assessments or mold remediation.

What we check

Every accessible room

Walls, ceilings, floors, baseboards, under sinks, showers, kitchen and laundry.

Thermal imaging

Temperature patterns around windows, plumbing and the roofline that can point to hidden moisture.

AC system & attic

Air handler, coil, drain pan and line, duct connections and attic insulation.

Lab-analyzed samples

Up to 3 air or surface samples with an outdoor control, sent to an accredited lab.
