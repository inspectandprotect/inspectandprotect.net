Florida Home Buyer's Inspection Checklist

What to look at, and what to line up, during your inspection period.

Book early in your inspection period

Florida contracts often give buyers a short inspection window. Book the inspection right away so there's time for follow-ups, repair quotes and insurance quotes.

Get insurance forms in the same visit

Your insurer may need a 4-point and will usually give credit for a wind mitigation form. Getting both during the home inspection means you can shop for insurance before your deadline.

What matters most in Florida homes

Roof age and condition, the AC system and its drain line, signs of moisture and past leaks, the electrical panel, and the type of plumbing. These drive both repair costs and insurability.

Attend if you can

Walking the home with the inspector is the fastest way to understand what's serious and what's routine maintenance.
